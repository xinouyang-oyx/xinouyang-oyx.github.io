Xin Ouyang

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Economics and Management, Tongji University. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the Institute of Economics at Tsinghua University and was a visiting scholar at the Center for Labor and a Just Economy at Harvard University.

My research uses causal inference and AI-based methods to study how emerging technologies and institutional change shape technological innovation and inequality.

Research Fields: Technological Innovation | Strategy | Inequality | AI & Digital Economy
